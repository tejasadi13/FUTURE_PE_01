# Lovable Website Prompt

Build a professional coaching center website called "ElevateIQ Academy" based in Hyderabad, India.

## Design

- Clean, modern, professional look
- Color scheme: Deep navy blue `#0A1628` and gold/amber `#F5A623` with white
- Font: Modern sans-serif, large bold headings
- Fully responsive on mobile and desktop
- Smooth scroll navigation

## Pages / Sections

Create a single-page scrolling website with these sections:

1. Navbar
   - Logo: ElevateIQ Academy
   - Nav links: Home, Courses, Why Us, Contact
   - CTA button: Book Free Demo

2. Hero Section
   - Headline: Crack JEE, NEET, UPSC & CAT — Right Here in Hyderabad.
   - Sub-headline: Small batches. Expert faculty. Real results — for students who mean business.
   - Body: At ElevateIQ Academy, we run focused batches of no more than 20 students per class. Our faculty includes IIT and IIM graduates who teach you how to think, strategize, and outperform under exam pressure.
   - Buttons: Book Your Free Demo Class, View Our Courses

3. Stats Bar
   - 20 students per batch max
   - Experienced Hyderabad faculty
   - Students guided across exam tracks

4. Courses Section
   - JEE Mains + Advanced
   - NEET Coaching
   - UPSC Civil Services
   - CAT & MBA Entrance
   - Crash Courses

5. Mid-Page CTA
   - Heading: See How We Teach. Before You Decide.
   - Body: Sit in on a real class — no commitment, no pressure.
   - Button: Book a Free Demo Class

6. Why Us Section
   - Small batches
   - Expert faculty
   - Structured exam practice
   - Personal mentorship

7. Urgency CTA Section
   - Heading: 2025–26 Batches Are Filling Fast.
   - Body: We keep our batches small by design — 20 seats per batch, no exceptions.
   - Button: Check Seat Availability

8. Contact / Footer CTA
   - Heading: Have Questions? Talk to Us Directly.
