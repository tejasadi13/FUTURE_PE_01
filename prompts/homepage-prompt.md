# Homepage Copy Prompt

Using the business profile below, generate homepage copy for a local cafe website.

Business name: Dyu Art Cafe  
Business type: Cafe and casual dining space  
Location: Koramangala, Bangalore  
Audience: Students, working professionals, friends, couples, artists, readers, and visitors  
Tone: Warm, friendly, calm, creative, and simple  
Main benefit: A peaceful cafe experience where food, conversations, and creative atmosphere come together  

Create:

1. A strong homepage headline with a clear value proposition
2. A sub-headline explaining who the cafe is for and why they should visit
3. A short intro section
4. Three benefit points
5. A short "Why Visit Us" section

Rules:

- Keep the language simple and publish-ready
- Make the copy specific to Koramangala and cafe visitors
- Avoid fake offers, phone numbers, or unsupported claims
- Make it sound like a real local business website
