# Homepage Copy Prompt

Using the business profile below, generate homepage copy for a local coaching center website.

Business name: BrightPath Coaching Centre  
Business type: Local coaching and test preparation center  
Location: Indiranagar, Bangalore  
Audience: Class 9 to 12 students, college students, exam aspirants, and parents  
Tone: Professional, supportive, clear, encouraging, and trustworthy  
Main benefit: Structured coaching that helps students understand concepts, stay consistent, and prepare confidently for exams  

Create:

1. A strong homepage headline with a clear value proposition
2. A sub-headline explaining who the coaching center is for and why they should inquire
3. A short intro section
4. Three benefit points
5. A short "Why Choose Us" section

Rules:

- Keep the language simple and publish-ready
- Make the copy specific to students, parents, and Indiranagar
- Avoid fake results, rank promises, or unsupported claims
- Make it sound like a real local coaching center website
