Audience: JEE, NEET, UPSC, and CAT aspirants, parents, and working professionals

Create:

1. A strong homepage headline with a clear value proposition
2. A sub-headline explaining who the academy is for and why they should inquire
3. A short intro section

Rules:

- Keep the language simple and publish-ready
